# TurtleBot3 Burger visual mesh

`burger_base.obj`, `left_tire.obj`, and `right_tire.obj` were converted from the
corresponding STL files in the [ROBOTIS TurtleBot3 repository](https://github.com/ROBOTIS-GIT/turtlebot3/tree/main/turtlebot3_description/meshes).
Copyright ROBOTIS Co., Ltd. The source repository is licensed under Apache-2.0;
see its [LICENSE](https://github.com/ROBOTIS-GIT/turtlebot3/blob/main/LICENSE).
The meshes were scaled from millimetres to metres and reoriented for Unity.

These are the official ROBOTIS Burger meshes, sourced from ROBOTIS's repository.
They are not the separate Thingiverse CC BY upload shown in the user's listing.
